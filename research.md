## Judge-seat critique of the top three

- #1 (pen): brief fits on first read; the laugh is the contradiction itself, with no tag after it; the fix answers a dying pen by giving it a formal exit, using the one faculty it has. Risk: "mid-word" is a slightly fussy setup, but it keeps the scene concrete and under 220 chars.
- #2 (button): clean one sentence and an exact bureaucratic frame for a fake button, but "false advertising" is a known punchline; on C2 and C4 it trails #1.
- #3 (crossing): ticket-for-loitering is a decent reversal, but the annoyance is less universal than a pen drying up and a fine punishes the pedestrian instead of dealing with the useless button; on C3 it trails #1.
